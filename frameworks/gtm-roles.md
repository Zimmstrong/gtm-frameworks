# GTM Roles Frameworks for B2B SaaS

> Map every role to a stage of the customer journey.

Whiteboard version: https://claude.ai/artifact/BpoqCVVvEvpuo6PUnTvKcK

## 1. Who owns each stage of the customer journey

| Stage | Owner(s) | Side |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Marketing / Demand Gen | Acquire (pre-sale) |
| Lead → MQL | Marketing Ops, Growth | Acquire |
| Qualify → SQL | SDR / BDR | Acquire |
| Discover & Demo | AE + Solutions Engineer | Acquire |
| Close | AE, Deal Desk, Sales Manager | Acquire |
| Onboard | Implementation / Onboarding | Retain (post-sale) |
| Adopt, Expand, Renew | CSM + Account Manager | Retain & expand |

RevOps supports every stage. The **AE → Onboarding/CSM hand-off** is where revenue most often leaks.

## 2. Team models

| Model | How it works | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assembly line** (Predictable Revenue) | SDR → AE → CSM, each a specialist | Scales; clear metrics per role | Friction at every hand-off |
| **Pods** | A small team (SDR, AE, SE, CSM) owns one segment or territory end to end | Tight collaboration, shared goal | Harder to balance workload |
| **Full-cycle AE** | One rep prospects, closes and often expands the account | Continuity for the buyer | Prospecting slips when the rep is busy closing |
| **PLG + sales assist** | The product drives sign-ups; sales steps in on high-intent accounts | Efficient acquisition cost | Needs reliable product-usage data |
